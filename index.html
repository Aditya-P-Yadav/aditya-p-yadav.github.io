Analytics and consultancy project

From Operational Record to Evidence Base

A five person consultancy engagement for the Research Skills Team inside Libraries and Learning Resources at the University of Birmingham. A live client, their own data, their own deadline. The brief did not say what to build.

June to September 2026Team of fivePython, Tableau, forecasting

The headline said attendance had fallen 48 percent.It had not. The records had.

Forty seven of the 399 sessions had no attendance recorded at all, and only a quarter had a school recorded against them. Once the missing records were accounted for, average attendance per session was almost unchanged. The collapse was in the recording, not in the room.

Overall satisfaction

The real requirement

No KPIs existed, so we agreed them from scratch with the client. The open brief became three ranked workstreams: workshop performance, a reporting asset the team could maintain themselves, and where engagement was missing.

The scope line

Partway through, the remit moved from describing what had happened to predicting what happens next. We put the change in front of the client, agreed what it meant for the deadline, and re-set the scope in writing.

The handover

1,878 records from 2023 to 2026 merged and cleaned in Python, six Tableau dashboards, exponential smoothing forecasts, and a documented pipeline so the team can rerun it without me.
